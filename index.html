Mosman RSL sub-Branch

Our mission

To support the wellbeing of veterans and their families in Mosman, to commemorate the service and sacrifice of Australians in war and peacekeeping, and to bring the community together in remembrance.

Mosman RSL sub-Branch is a local sub-Branch of The Returned and Services League of Australia (New South Wales Branch), known as RSL NSW. We are run by volunteers, many of them veterans themselves, and we work alongside RSL NSW, the Department of Veterans' Affairs and local organisations to make sure no veteran in our area is left without support.

The sub-Branch is a not-for-profit organisation registered with the Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission (ACNC). Our full registration details are listed below.

What we do

Commemoration

We organise Mosman's ANZAC Day Dawn Service at the Georges Heights Parade Ground and the Remembrance Day service each November, inviting the community, schools and local dignitaries to remember those who served.

Veteran welfare

We visit and keep in contact with veterans and war widows in our area, including those in hospital or aged care, and help them connect with the support they are entitled to.

Advocacy & support

We help veterans and families find their way to RSL NSW advocacy services and the Department of Veterans' Affairs for claims, entitlements and wellbeing support.

Community & youth

We work with local schools and community groups to pass on the stories of Mosman's servicemen and women to the next generation.

Our history

Mosman RSL sub-Branch has served veterans and the Mosman community from its home on Military Road, and is registered as a charity with the ACNC since 3 December 2012.

Mosman sent many of its sons and daughters to the First World War. Their stories are documented by the community history project Mosman 1914–1918.

Our impact

Each year the sub-Branch leads Mosman's ANZAC Day Dawn Service and Remembrance Day service, bringing together veterans, serving members, families, schools and local dignitaries. Throughout the year our volunteers keep in touch with local veterans and war widows and connect them with RSL NSW and Department of Veterans' Affairs support.
